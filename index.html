The loop

Four moves between surplus and a served plate

Every rescue follows the same choreographed path, and every participant sees the same clock. No phone tag, no spreadsheets, no guessing whether the food is still safe.

Post the surplus

A kitchen logs quantity, storage temperature and a safety window. The listing is live in under 40 seconds.

Match a receiver

Nearby shelters see it ranked by urgency and distance, with capacity already accounted for.

Dispatch a courier

A volunteer accepts, the pickup is tracked, and the safety countdown follows the food, not the paperwork.

Confirm the impact

Delivery is signed off, servings are logged, and the city dashboard updates for everyone instantly.

Safety-window countdown

Every listing carries a live clock tied to its storage class. Colour, motion and sort order all shift as the window closes, so urgency is impossible to miss.

SOS beacon protocol

When a rescue drops under an hour, one tap broadcasts it to every verified receiver and courier in range, pinning it to the top of every board in the city.
